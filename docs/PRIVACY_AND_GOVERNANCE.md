# Privacy and Governance

Use only authorized, case-associated images and CCTV. The system follows minimum-necessary handling: parents see their own case’s safe status; police/reviewers see authorized operational detail; evidence stays in controlled storage; and notifications never expose parent-inappropriate evidence or scores.

Audit records identify human or SYSTEM/AI activity without storing passwords, tokens, embeddings, or image contents. Retention is report-first and deletion requires explicit authorization; actual organizational retention periods must be set under applicable policy and law. A last observed camera is a CCTV observation, not GPS or live tracking.

This is a research/student prototype and not a certified law-enforcement platform. It does not claim GDPR, DPDP Act, police-regulation, or other legal compliance.
